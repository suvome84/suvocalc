Frequently Asked Questions

Is SuvoCalc really free?

Yes. All 57 calculators are free to use, with no charges, no subscriptions and no locked features.

Do I need an account to use SuvoCalc?

No. There is no login and no account. You can open any calculator and use it straight away.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Calculations happen in your browser and the numbers you enter are not sent to a server or stored online. Only your theme choice and a list of calculators you recently used are kept on your own device, and you can clear them by clearing your browser data.

Which calculators are included?

57 calculators across finance, tax, investment, health, education, date and time, and daily utilities — including EMI, GST, SIP, income tax, BMI, CGPA, age, discount, and a scientific calculator.

How accurate are the results?

Results use standard formulas and current Indian rates where they apply, such as the GST 2.0 slabs of 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%. They are meant as a guide. For a loan, tax filing or investment decision, check the figures with the bank, the official tax portal, or a qualified adviser.

Does SuvoCalc work offline?

Once a calculator page has loaded, it works without an internet connection, because the calculation runs in your browser. You need to be online the first time you open a page.
