Date Difference Calculator

The Date Difference Calculator finds the exact number of days, weeks, and months between any two dates.

What it is used for

It is used to count the days, weeks, or months between two dates — for deadlines, countdowns, age gaps, project planning, and leave calculations.

Date Difference

—

Formula

Days = absolute difference between the two dates, in whole days. Weeks = days ÷ 7. Years and months = calendar difference between the two dates. Business days (approx) = days × 5 ÷ 7.

Worked example

Inputs: Start Date 1 January 2026, End Date 21 November 2026. Step 1: count the days between the two dates: 324 days. Step 2: whole weeks: 324 ÷ 7 = 46 weeks with 2 days left over. Step 3: months: 10 months. Step 4: business days (approx): 324 × 5 ÷ 7 ≈ 231. Final result: 324 days, 10 months, 46 weeks, about 231 business days.

How to use

  1. Tap the Start Date field and pick the first date from the calendar.
  2. Tap the End Date field and pick the second date.
  3. Tap Calculate to see the difference.
  4. Read the result in days, years and months, and total weeks.
  5. Use the approximate business-days figure when you need a working-days estimate.

Tips

  • Use it to count days to a deadline, an exam, or a birthday.
  • For project planning, use the business-days figure for work estimates and the total days for calendar dates.
  • If you need the difference including both the start and end day, add 1 to the result.
  • Check the year first if the answer looks far too large or too small.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the dates the wrong way round and thinking the result is negative — the tool always gives a positive difference.
  • Counting the start and end days twice when adding the difference to a deadline.
  • Treating the approximate business-days figure as exact when holidays fall in between.
  • Forgetting leap years when counting long gaps by hand.

Frequently asked questions

Does the order of the two dates matter?

It counts the number of days between the two dates. Changing the order does not change the answer, because the tool uses the absolute difference.

How are business days counted?

The business-day figure is an estimate: total days × 5 ÷ 7. It does not remove public holidays, so treat it as approximate.

Can I enter a date like 31 February?

No, the tool only accepts dates from the date picker, which keeps the month lengths and leap years correct automatically.
