Tip Calculator

The Tip Calculator works out the tip amount, the total bill, and how much each person pays when the bill is split.

What it is used for

It is used at restaurants and cafes to calculate the tip, the total to pay, and each person's share when splitting the bill.

Tip Amount

—

Formula

Tip Amount = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100. Total = Bill Amount + Tip Amount. Per Person = Total ÷ Number of People.

Worked example

Inputs: Bill Amount ₹1,200, Tip Percentage 15%, Split Between 4 people. Step 1: tip = 1,200 × 15 ÷ 100 = ₹180. Step 2: total = 1,200 + 180 = ₹1,380. Step 3: per person = 1,380 ÷ 4 = ₹345. Final result: tip ₹180, total ₹1,380, each person pays ₹345.

How to use

  1. Enter the bill total in the Bill Amount (₹) field, for example 1200.
  2. Enter the tip percentage in the Tip Percentage (%) field, for example 15.
  3. Enter the number of people in the Split Between (people) field, for example 4.
  4. Tap Calculate to see the tip amount.
  5. Read the total bill and the per-person share shown under the result.

Tips

  • Check the bill for a service charge before adding a tip.
  • Fifteen percent is a generous tip for good service; ten percent is a common standard.
  • Round each person's share up to the nearest ₹10 to make settling up easy.
  • If one person had a much bigger order, split by what each person ordered instead of equally.

Common mistakes

  • Tipping on a bill that already includes a service charge, which means paying twice.
  • Forgetting to change the tip percentage from the default when you want a different rate.
  • Dividing the bill before adding the tip, which leaves the tip out of each person's share.
  • Rounding each person's share down and ending up short of the total.

Frequently asked questions

What is a normal tip percentage?

Ten percent is a common starting point in India, and the tool defaults to 15% in its tip field. Check the bill first — some restaurants already add a service charge.

Can I split the bill between friends?

Yes. Set Split Between to the number of people and the tool shows what each person pays including the tip.

Is the tip calculated before or after tax?

The tip is calculated on the bill amount you enter. If the bill already includes tax, the tip is calculated on that total.
