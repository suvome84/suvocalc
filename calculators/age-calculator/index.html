Age Calculator

The Age Calculator works out your exact age in years, months, and days from your date of birth, along with the total days you have lived.

What it is used for

It is used to find an exact age for forms, birthdays, school or job eligibility, anniversaries, and to count down the days to your next birthday.

Your Age

—

Formula

Age = today's date minus date of birth, borrowing days and months like ordinary subtraction. It also counts total days lived and days until the next birthday.

Worked example

Inputs: Date of Birth 21 November 2004, calculated on 21 November 2026. Step 1: years: 2026 − 2004 = 22. Step 2: months and days: same day and month, so 0 months and 0 days. Step 3: total days lived: 8,035 days, which is 1,147 weeks. Final result: 22 years, 0 months, 0 days old, with the next birthday in 365 days.

How to use

  1. Tap the Date of Birth field and pick your birth date from the calendar.
  2. Check the date is correct — a wrong year changes everything.
  3. Tap Calculate to see your age in years, months, and days.
  4. Read the total days lived and total weeks shown under the result.
  5. Note the days until your next birthday if you are planning ahead.

Tips

  • For official forms, use the date of birth exactly as it appears on your birth certificate or ID.
  • If you were born on 29 February, your birthday falls on 28 February or 1 March in non-leap years depending on local convention — check the document that needs the date.
  • The total-days figure is handy for counting anniversaries in days, for example 10,000 days old.
  • Re-check the year first if the result looks wrong — a one-digit slip in the year is the usual cause.

Common mistakes

  • Picking the wrong year in the date picker, especially when scrolling back many years.
  • Entering today's date instead of the birth date, which gives an age of zero.
  • Expecting the calculator to accept a future date of birth — it rejects dates in the future.
  • Confusing total days lived with age in years; they are different numbers for a reason.

Frequently asked questions

Can I calculate the age of someone else?

Yes. Enter any past date of birth and the calculator works out that person's age as of today.

Why does the age show months and days as well as years?

Because two people born in the same year can be months apart in age. The months and days make the result exact rather than rounded.

What happens if I enter a future date?

The calculator shows an error. A date of birth cannot be in the future.
