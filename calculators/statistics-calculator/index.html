Statistics Calculator

The Statistics Calculator finds the mean, median, mode, range, variance, and standard deviation of any set of numbers you type in.

What it is used for

It is used by students and anyone working with data to summarise a set of numbers — exam marks, measurements, or survey results — with one average, one centre, and one measure of spread.

Mean (Avg)

—

Formula

Mean = sum of all numbers ÷ count. Median = middle value of the sorted list (or the average of the two middle values). Mode = most frequent value. Range = largest − smallest. Variance = average of (each number − mean)². Standard deviation = square root of variance.

Worked example

Inputs: Numbers 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42. Step 1: sort them: 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42. Step 2: sum = 4 + 8 + 15 + 16 + 23 + 42 = 108. Step 3: mean = 108 ÷ 6 = 18. Step 4: median = middle two averaged: (15 + 16) ÷ 2 = 15.5. Step 5: range = 42 − 4 = 38. Step 6: variance = average of squared differences from the mean = 151.7; standard deviation = √151.7 = 12.32. Final result: mean 18, median 15.5, no mode, range 38, variance 151.7, standard deviation 12.32.

How to use

  1. Type your numbers in the Numbers (comma-separated) field, for example 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42.
  2. Separate each number with a comma and a space.
  3. The result updates as you type.
  4. Read the mean, median, mode, range, variance, and standard deviation.
  5. Check that you typed every number — one missing value changes all the results.

Tips

  • Sort the numbers mentally before checking the median — the middle of an unsorted list is not the median.
  • With an even count of numbers, the median is the average of the two middle values.
  • Use the median for incomes and house prices, where a few huge values distort the mean.
  • Standard deviation is most useful when comparing two sets: the one with the smaller value is more consistent.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting a comma between two numbers, so they join into one wrong number.
  • Using the mean when one extreme value skews it — the median is the better centre then.
  • Confusing variance and standard deviation — standard deviation is the square root of variance.
  • Entering text or symbols among the numbers, which the tool cannot read.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between mean and median?

The mean is the average — add all numbers and divide by how many there are. The median is the middle value when the numbers are sorted. The median is less affected by one very large or very small number.

What is the mode?

The mode is the number that appears most often. If every number appears once, as in the example, there is no mode.

What does standard deviation tell me?

Standard deviation measures how spread out the numbers are around the mean. A small value means the numbers are close together; a large value means they are spread out.
