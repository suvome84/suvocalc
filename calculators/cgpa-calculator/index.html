CGPA / GPA Calculator

The CGPA Calculator averages your semester SGPAs into one CGPA, and converts it to a percentage using the common × 9.5 rule.

What it is used for

It is used by college students to work out their overall CGPA from semester SGPAs, and to get a rough percentage equivalent for resumes and applications.

CGPA

—

Formula

CGPA = (SGPA of semester 1 + SGPA of semester 2 + …) ÷ number of semesters entered. Percentage (tool's conversion) = CGPA × 9.5. Grade scale used by the tool: 9 and above O (Outstanding), 8–9 A+ (Excellent), 7–8 A (Very Good), 6–7 B+ (Good), below 6 B (Average).

Worked example

Inputs: Sem 1 SGPA 8.5, Sem 2 SGPA 8.2, Sem 3 SGPA 8.7, Sem 4 SGPA 9.0. Step 1: add the SGPAs: 8.5 + 8.2 + 8.7 + 9.0 = 34.4. Step 2: divide by the number of semesters: 34.4 ÷ 4 = 8.6. Step 3: percentage = 8.6 × 9.5 = 81.7%. Final result: CGPA 8.6, about 81.7%, graded A+ (Excellent) by the tool.

How to use

  1. Enter each completed semester's SGPA in the Sem 1 SGPA, Sem 2 SGPA, and Sem 3 SGPA fields.
  2. Enter the Sem 4 SGPA (optional) field too if you have a fourth semester, or leave it blank.
  3. Tap Calculate to see your CGPA.
  4. Read the percentage and grade shown under the result.
  5. Check your university's own conversion rule before writing the percentage on any official form.

Tips

  • Only include completed semesters; the calculator ignores blanks, not zeros.
  • If one semester had a much lower SGPA, check whether retaking or improving later semesters can pull the average up.
  • Keep your SGPA values from the official result sheet, not from memory.
  • A CGPA of 8 or above is usually considered strong for most Indian university placements.

Common mistakes

  • Averaging in a blank semester as zero, which drags the CGPA down — leave unfinished semesters blank.
  • Entering percentage marks instead of SGPA values.
  • Treating the × 9.5 percentage as official for every university — the rule varies.
  • Forgetting that a credit-weighted average is different from a simple average when semesters carry different credits.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use it if I have only finished two semesters?

Enter your SGPA for each semester. Leave any semester you have not completed blank — the calculator only averages the ones you entered.

Is CGPA × 9.5 the official percentage formula?

The tool uses CGPA × 9.5, a common conversion used by many Indian universities. But the exact rule varies — always check your own university's official formula before using the percentage on a form.

Does this calculator weight semesters by credits?

No, CGPA is normally the average of SGPA across semesters when each semester carries equal credit weight. If your semesters have different credits, the proper method is a credit-weighted average, which this tool does not do.
