Electricity Bill Calculator

The Electricity Bill Calculator estimates your monthly electricity bill from the units consumed, the rate per unit, and any fixed charges.

What it is used for

It is used to estimate the monthly electricity cost before the bill arrives, and to see the daily average usage and daily cost.

Monthly Bill

—

Formula

Monthly Bill = Units Consumed (kWh) × Rate per Unit (₹) + Fixed Charges (₹). Daily average units = units ÷ 30. Daily cost = bill ÷ 30.

Worked example

Inputs: Units Consumed 250 kWh, Rate per Unit ₹6.50, Fixed Charges ₹100. Step 1: energy charge = 250 × 6.50 = ₹1,625. Step 2: add fixed charges: 1,625 + 100 = ₹1,725. Step 3: daily average = 250 ÷ 30 = 8.3 kWh a day; daily cost = 1,725 ÷ 30 = ₹57.50 a day. Final result: monthly bill ₹1,725.

How to use

  1. Enter the units used in the Units Consumed (kWh) field, for example 250.
  2. Enter the price of one unit in the Rate per Unit (₹) field, for example 6.5.
  3. Enter any flat monthly amount in the Fixed Charges (₹) field, for example 100.
  4. Tap Calculate to see the monthly bill.
  5. Read the daily average units and daily cost shown under the result.

Tips

  • Compare the calculator's result with your last real bill to see how close the flat-rate estimate is.
  • If your bill uses slab rates, enter the average rate per unit from your bill for a closer estimate.
  • A sudden jump in units usually points to a faulty appliance, an old fridge, or a water heater left on.
  • Running heavy appliances like ACs and heaters fewer hours is the fastest way to cut units.

Common mistakes

  • Using the sanctioned load or the meter reading itself instead of the units consumed that month.
  • Entering the rate in paise instead of rupees, which makes the bill 100 times too large.
  • Leaving fixed charges at zero when your bill always includes them.
  • Expecting an exact match with a slab-based bill — this tool uses one flat rate.

Frequently asked questions

Does this match my actual electricity bill?

It is a simple estimate: units × rate + fixed charges. Real bills use slab rates, where the price per unit changes as usage rises, plus taxes and meter rent — so the calculator's figure can differ from the actual bill.

How do I find the units consumed?

Read the meter at the start and end of the month and subtract the two readings. The difference is the units consumed.

What are fixed charges?

Fixed charges are the flat monthly amount the supplier charges regardless of usage, such as meter rent or a service fee. Check your bill for the exact figure.
