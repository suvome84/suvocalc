Grade Calculator

The Grade Calculator turns marks obtained out of total marks into a percentage, a letter grade, and an approximate CGPA.

What it is used for

It is used by students to find the letter grade and percentage for exam marks, check pass or fail, and get a rough CGPA equivalent.

Grade

—

Formula

Percentage = marks obtained ÷ total marks × 100. Grade scale used by the tool: 90%+ A+, 80–89% A, 70–79% B+, 60–69% B, 50–59% C, 40–49% D, below 40% F. Approximate CGPA = percentage ÷ 9.5. Pass mark used by the tool: 35%.

Worked example

Inputs: Marks Obtained 78, Total Marks 100. Step 1: percentage = 78 ÷ 100 × 100 = 78%. Step 2: the tool's grade scale gives 78% a grade of B+ (70–79%). Step 3: approximate CGPA = 78 ÷ 9.5 = 8.21. Final result: Grade B+, 78%, 78 out of 100, and a pass (the tool's pass mark is 35%).

How to use

  1. Enter your marks in the Marks Obtained field, for example 78.
  2. Enter the full marks in the Total Marks field, for example 100.
  3. If you want, add a second subject in the Subject 2 (optional) field.
  4. Tap Calculate to see your grade.
  5. Read the percentage, the approximate CGPA, and the pass or fail result shown under the grade.

Tips

  • Check your board's own grade boundaries — a B+ in one board may be an A in another.
  • For a pass or fail, 35% is the tool's mark, but your school may set a different one.
  • Keep the marks sheet handy and copy the numbers exactly; one wrong digit changes the grade.
  • Use the percentage on applications that ask for a number, and the letter grade only where a grade is asked for.

Common mistakes

  • Entering marks obtained greater than total marks, which the calculator rejects.
  • Using the marks of one subject against the total of all subjects.
  • Treating the tool's grade scale as your board's official scale — it varies by board and school.
  • Rounding the percentage before calculating, which can push a borderline grade the wrong way.

Frequently asked questions

What grade do I get for 85%?

The tool's scale is: 90% and above A+, 80–89% A, 70–79% B+, 60–69% B, 50–59% C, 40–49% D, below 40% F. Schools and boards use their own scales, so this is a general guide.

Can I add a second subject?

Yes — type the second subject's marks in the Subject 2 field in the same marks-out-of-total style, for example 85/100.

What is the difference between a grade and a percentage?

A grade is a letter band for one result, while a percentage is the exact number. The calculator shows both so you can use whichever your form asks for.
