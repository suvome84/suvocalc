Time Zone Converter

The Time Zone Converter converts a date and time between 15 major world time zones, using their fixed UTC offsets.

What it is used for

It is used to convert meeting times, flight times, and call times between time zones, so everyone joins at the right local hour.

Formula

Converted time = your time + (offset of the To zone − offset of the From zone). Offsets used by the tool: UTC+0, IST +5:30, EST −5, PST −8, CST −6, GMT +0, CET +1, JST +9, AEST +10, BDT +6, PKT +5, MST −7, BST +1, SGT +8, HKT +8.

Worked example

Inputs: Your Time 10:00, From Zone IST (UTC+5:30), To Zone EST (UTC−5). Step 1: the offset difference is 5.5 − (−5) = 10.5 hours. Step 2: subtract 10.5 hours from 10:00, which gives 23:30 on the previous day. Final result: 10:00 IST is 23:30 EST the previous day.

How to use

  1. Enter your date and time in the Your Time field.
  2. Choose the starting zone in the From Zone dropdown, for example IST (UTC+5:30).
  3. Choose the target zone in the To Zone dropdown, for example EST (UTC−5).
  4. Tap Convert to see the time in the other zone.
  5. Check the date as well as the time — the result can fall on the previous or next day.

Tips

  • For meetings, confirm the time with the other person in their own local time to avoid DST surprises.
  • IST is always UTC+5:30 and never changes, which makes it a reliable anchor zone.
  • When the converted time crosses midnight, note the new date clearly in your message.
  • For exact scheduling with DST zones, cross-check with a clock that knows the current DST rules.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting that zones with daylight saving time shift by an hour in summer — the tool uses fixed offsets only.
  • Mixing up AM and PM when entering the time.
  • Assuming the date stays the same when the converted time crosses midnight.
  • Choosing the wrong zone, for example CST (US Central) instead of IST, when the abbreviations look similar.

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle daylight saving time?

The tool uses fixed offsets for each zone, such as IST at UTC+5:30 and EST at UTC−5. It does not adjust for daylight saving time, so zones that observe DST can be off by an hour during part of the year.

Which time zones can I choose?

The tool lists 15 zones: UTC, IST, EST, PST, CST, GMT, CET, JST, AEST, BDT, PKT, MST, BST, SGT, and HKT.

Can the converted time be on a different day?

The converted time may fall on a different date. Read the full result, which shows the date change when it happens.
