Unit & Currency Converter

The Unit & Currency Converter converts between units of length, weight, and temperature, and between currencies using built-in approximate exchange rates.

What it is used for

It is used to convert everyday measurements — metres to feet, kilograms to pounds, Celsius to Fahrenheit — and to get a quick approximate currency conversion.

Formula

Length: 1 km = 1,000 m; 1 m = 100 cm; 1 inch = 2.54 cm; 1 foot = 12 inches; 1 mile = 1.60934 km. Weight: 1 kg = 2.20462 lb; 1 kg = 1,000 g. Temperature: °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32; °C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9. Currency: converted amount = amount ÷ rate of the From currency × rate of the To currency, using the tool's built-in approximate rates (for example INR 83.5 per USD).

Worked example

Currency: convert ₹1,000 to US dollars using the tool's built-in rate table (₹83.50 per US dollar). Step 1: 1,000 ÷ 83.5 = 11.98. Final result: about $11.98. Length: 5 kilometres = 5,000 metres. Weight: 70 kg = 154.32 pounds. Temperature: 25°C = 77°F (25 × 9 ÷ 5 + 32). Note that the tool's currency rates are fixed approximate rates, so check your bank's live rate before any real payment.

How to use

  1. Tap the tab for what you want to convert: Currency, Length, Weight, or Temp.
  2. Choose the From unit and the To unit.
  3. Enter the amount to convert.
  4. Read the converted value shown below.
  5. For currency, remember the rate is approximate — confirm the live rate before paying.

Tips

  • For shopping, convert the price into your home currency before deciding if a deal is good.
  • For cooking, convert grams to cups only with a recipe's own table — weight and volume are not the same.
  • When travelling, save the live rate from your bank and compare it with the tool's approximate rate.
  • Double-check the units in the From and To boxes before reading the answer.

Common mistakes

  • Trusting the built-in currency rate for a real payment — it is approximate and can be out of date.
  • Mixing up similar units, such as US and UK gallons or pounds weight and pounds money.
  • Entering a temperature in the wrong scale, for example typing 25°F when you mean 25°C.
  • Forgetting that the tool's currency table is fixed and does not update with the market.

Frequently asked questions

Are the conversion rates accurate?

The length, weight, and temperature conversions are exact standard conversions. The currency rates are fixed approximate values stored in the tool, so they can differ from the live market rate — check your bank before paying.

What can I convert?

The four tabs are Currency, Length, Weight, and Temp. Pick the tab, then choose the units and enter the amount.

Can I convert dollars to rupees as well?

Yes, you can convert in either direction — just swap the From and To units.

Why is temperature conversion different?

No. Temperature conversion uses a formula, not a multiplier, because Celsius and Fahrenheit start at different zero points.
