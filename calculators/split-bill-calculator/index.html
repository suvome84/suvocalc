Split Bill

The Split Bill Calculator divides a restaurant bill equally between people, including a tip percentage of your choice.

What it is used for

It is used after a group meal to split the bill fairly, including tip, so everyone knows exactly what to pay.

Each Person Pays

—

Formula

Tip Amount = Total Bill × Tip % ÷ 100. Total = Total Bill + Tip Amount. Per Person = Total ÷ Number of People.

Worked example

Inputs: Total Bill ₹1,200, Number of People 4, Tip 10%. Step 1: tip = 1,200 × 10 ÷ 100 = ₹120. Step 2: total with tip = 1,200 + 120 = ₹1,320. Step 3: per person = 1,320 ÷ 4 = ₹330. Final result: total ₹1,320, each person pays ₹330.

How to use

  1. Enter the full bill in the Total Bill (₹) field, for example 1200.
  2. Enter the number of people in the Number of People field, for example 4.
  3. Enter the tip percentage in the Tip (%) field — it defaults to 10.
  4. Tap Split to see the result.
  5. Read the total with tip and what each person pays.

Tips

  • Check whether a service charge is already on the bill before adding a tip.
  • Round each person's share up to the nearest ₹10 so the total is covered.
  • If one person had a far bigger order, split that person's items separately instead of equally.
  • Set the tip to 0 when you are splitting a bill that is not a restaurant meal.

Common mistakes

  • Splitting before adding the tip, so the tip is left out of everyone's share.
  • Counting people who are not paying, which makes each share too small.
  • Forgetting that the tip field defaults to 10% and leaving it unchanged by accident.
  • Rounding each share down and ending up short of the bill total.

Frequently asked questions

Can I split without adding a tip?

Yes. Enter 0 in the Tip field and the tool splits the bill amount alone.

Does it split by what each person ordered?

No — it splits the bill equally. If people ordered very different amounts, split by each person's order instead.

What tip percentage does it use by default?

The Tip field defaults to 10%. Change it to any percentage, or to 0 for no tip.
