Speed / Distance / Time

The Speed / Distance / Time Calculator solves for whichever of the three is missing, and also works out running pace for a race distance and finish time.

What it is used for

It is used to plan travel time for a trip, work out a vehicle's average speed, and calculate running pace for 5 km, 10 km, half-marathon, and marathon distances.

Formula

Speed = Distance ÷ Time. Distance = Speed × Time. Time = Distance ÷ Speed. Pace (min/km) = total minutes ÷ distance in km. The tool has two modes: Speed/Distance/Time (leave one field blank) and Running Pace (race distance + target finish time).

Worked example

Speed mode: Distance 120 km, Time 2 hours, Speed left blank. Step 1: Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h. Final result: speed 60 km/h. Pace mode: Race Distance 5 km, Target Finish Time 00:25:00. Step 1: pace = 25 minutes ÷ 5 km = 5 minutes per km. Step 2: speed = 5 km ÷ (25 ÷ 60) hours = 12 km/h. Final result: pace 5:00 min/km, average speed 12 km/h.

How to use

  1. Choose the mode: Speed / Dist / Time, or Running Pace.
  2. In Speed mode, fill in any two of Speed (km/h), Distance (km), and Time (hours), and leave the third blank.
  3. Pick Metric (km, km/h) or Imperial (miles, mph) in the Unit System dropdown.
  4. Tap Calculate to see the missing value.
  5. In Pace mode, choose a race distance and enter your target finish time as hh:mm:ss instead.

Tips

  • For a road trip, divide the distance by your expected average speed to get a realistic travel time.
  • Remember that average speed includes stops — a 60 km/h average on a highway is faster driving than it sounds.
  • For runners, a 5:00 min/km pace equals 12 km/h; a 6:00 min/km pace equals 10 km/h.
  • Enter time as a decimal (1.5 hours = 1 hour 30 minutes) in speed mode.

Common mistakes

  • Filling in all three fields, so the tool has nothing to calculate.
  • Mixing minutes and hours in the Time field — enter time in hours, for example 1.5 for 1 hour 30 minutes.
  • Using pace mode numbers in speed mode and getting a confusing answer.
  • Forgetting to switch the unit system when working in miles.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate speed if I know distance and time?

Leave exactly one of the three fields blank — Speed, Distance, or Time — and fill in the other two. The tool calculates the blank one.

What is the difference between pace and speed?

Pace is time per kilometre, for example 5 minutes per km. Speed is distance per hour, for example 12 km/h. They describe the same run from opposite sides.

Can I use miles instead of kilometres?

Yes. Switch the Unit System to Imperial (miles, mph) and the tool works in miles and miles per hour instead.
