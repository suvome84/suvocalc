Area Calculator

The Area Calculator works out the area and perimeter of squares, rectangles, triangles, circles, and trapezoids from their dimensions.

What it is used for

It is used to find the area of a floor, plot, wall, or garden, and the perimeter or circumference, for painting, flooring, fencing, and school geometry.

Area

—

Formula

Square: area = side², perimeter = 4 × side. Rectangle: area = length × width, perimeter = 2 × (length + width). Triangle: area = ½ × base × height. Circle: area = π × radius², circumference = 2 × π × radius. Trapezoid: area = ½ × (side a + side b) × height (c), where a and b are the two parallel sides and c is the height between them.

Worked example

Circle: choose Circle, enter Radius 10. Step 1: area = π × 10² = 3.14159 × 100 = 314.16 square units. Step 2: circumference = 2 × π × 10 = 62.83 units. Rectangle: choose Rectangle, enter Length 10 and Width 5. Area = 10 × 5 = 50 square units; perimeter = 2 × (10 + 5) = 30 units. Triangle: choose Triangle, enter Base 10 and Height 5. Area = ½ × 10 × 5 = 25 square units. Final results: circle 314.16, rectangle 50, triangle 25 square units. Trapezoid: choose Trapezoid, enter a = 10, b = 6, height (c) = 4. Area = ½ × (10 + 6) × 4 = 32 square units.

How to use

  1. Choose the shape in the Shape dropdown: Square, Rectangle, Triangle, Circle, or Trapezoid.
  2. Enter the first dimension in the Side / Length / Radius (a) field, for example 10.
  3. Enter the second dimension in the Width / Height / Top-Width (b) field when the shape needs one.
  4. For a trapezoid, also enter the height — the distance between the two parallel sides — in the Height (c) field.
  5. Tap Calculate to see the area.
  6. Read the perimeter or circumference shown under the result.

Tips

  • Measure twice before calculating — a wrong length gives a wrong area no matter how good the maths is.
  • For a room, multiply length by width in metres to get the floor area in square metres.
  • For a circle, measure across the middle and halve it to get the radius.
  • Keep all measurements in the same unit before entering them.

Common mistakes

  • Entering the diameter instead of the radius for a circle, which makes the area four times too large.
  • Mixing units, such as length in metres and width in centimetres.
  • Using the slant side of a triangle as its height.
  • Forgetting that area is in square units while perimeter is in plain units.

Frequently asked questions

Which shapes can I calculate?

Five shapes: Square, Rectangle, Triangle, Circle, and Trapezoid. Choose the shape first, then enter its dimensions.

How do I calculate a square?

Enter the side length once. Area = side × side and perimeter = 4 × side.

What do I enter for a trapezoid?

Enter the two parallel sides in fields (a) and (b), then enter the height — the distance between the two parallel sides — in field (c). Area = ½ × (a + b) × height.

Are the results in square metres?

The tool uses the units you enter, so keep them consistent — metres with metres, centimetres with centimetres — and the area comes out in square units of the same kind.
