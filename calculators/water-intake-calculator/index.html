Water Intake Calculator

The Water Intake Calculator recommends how much water to drink each day, based on your body weight, activity level, and climate.

What it is used for

It is used to set a daily drinking target in litres and glasses, so you can check whether you are drinking enough water for your weight and lifestyle.

Daily Water Intake

—

Formula

Daily water (ml) = weight (kg) × ml per kg for your activity level + climate adjustment. Activity rates: Sedentary 30, Lightly Active 33, Moderately Active 36, Very Active 39, Athlete 44 ml per kg. Climate: Hot/Humid +500 ml, Cold −100 ml, Temperate 0 ml. Glasses = total ml ÷ 250.

Worked example

Inputs: Body Weight 70 kg, Activity Moderately Active (3–5 days/week), Climate Temperate. Step 1: base = 70 × 36 = 2520 ml. Step 2: climate adjustment = 0 ml. Step 3: total = 2520 ml = 2.52 litres. Final result: about 2.5 litres a day, which is about 10 glasses of 250 ml. This is a general guide, not medical advice.

How to use

  1. Enter your body weight in the Body Weight (kg) field, for example 70.
  2. Choose your Activity Level from the dropdown, from Sedentary to Athlete.
  3. Choose your Climate: Temperate, Hot/Humid, or Cold.
  4. Tap Calculate to see your daily target in litres.
  5. Read the glasses count to turn the target into something easy to count through the day.

Tips

  • Spread the water through the day instead of drinking most of it at once.
  • Hot weather, sweating, exercise, and illness all raise your real need above the base figure.
  • Food also contains water — soups, fruit, and vegetables count toward hydration.
  • Keep a bottle at your desk or in your bag so the target is easy to reach.
  • If you have kidney, heart, or other medical conditions, follow your doctor's fluid advice instead of this calculator.

Common mistakes

  • Counting tea, coffee, and soft drinks as if they were plain water.
  • Drinking the whole target in one or two sittings and feeling bloated.
  • Using an old weight after gaining or losing several kilos.
  • Ignoring the climate setting when you live somewhere hot and humid.

Frequently asked questions

How many glasses of water should I drink a day?

It depends on your weight. For a 70 kg moderately active person the calculator gives about 2.5 litres, or about 10 glasses of 250 ml. Enter your own weight to get your figure.

Does exercise change the result?

Yes. Higher activity levels raise the ml-per-kg rate, from 30 ml per kg for sedentary up to 44 ml per kg for athletes.

Is it possible to drink too much water?

Yes, drinking far more than your body needs can be harmful. Use the calculator's figure as a guide and drink to thirst as well. If you have a medical condition, ask your doctor.
