Percentage Calculator

The Percentage Calculator finds what a percentage of any number is — for example, what 15% of 200 is — and also shows the value after that percentage is added or taken away.

What it is used for

It is used for everyday maths: working out discounts while shopping, calculating tax or a tip, finding marks as a percentage, and checking percentage increases or decreases.

Percentage Result

—

Formula

Result = Percentage ÷ 100 × Value. After an increase: Value + Result. After a decrease: Value − Result.

Worked example

Inputs: Percentage (%) = 15, Of Value = 200. Step 1: 15 ÷ 100 = 0.15. Step 2: 0.15 × 200 = 30. So 15% of 200 is ₹30. After a 15% increase the value is 200 + 30 = ₹230, and after a 15% decrease it is 200 − 30 = ₹170.

How to use

  1. Step 1: Enter the percentage in the "Percentage (%)" field — for example, 15.
  2. Step 2: Enter the number you want the percentage of in the "Of Value" field — for example, 200.
  3. Step 3: Tap Calculate to see the result.
  4. Step 4: Read the main result (15% of 200 = 30) and the extra lines showing the value after an increase and after a decrease.

Tips

  • To find a percentage increase, add the result to the original value; to find a discount, subtract it.
  • To work out what percentage one number is of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.
  • Remember that 50% is a half, 25% is a quarter, and 10% is a tenth — useful for quick mental checks.
  • When comparing two sale prices, always compare the final price after discount, not just the discount percentage.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing up the two fields — entering the value in the percentage box and the percentage in the value box.
  • Forgetting that a percentage is out of 100, so 15% of 200 is 30, not 15 × 200.
  • Applying a discount percentage to the already-discounted price instead of the original price.
  • Confusing "15% off" (pay 85%) with "pay 15%" — they are opposite amounts.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate 15% of 200?

Divide 15 by 100 to get 0.15, then multiply by 200. The answer is 30. You can do the same in this calculator by entering 15 in "Percentage (%)" and 200 in "Of Value".

What is the difference between percentage increase and percentage decrease?

A percentage increase adds the percentage amount to the original value, while a percentage decrease subtracts it. For example, 200 increased by 15% is 230, and 200 decreased by 15% is 170.

How do I find what percentage 30 is of 200?

Divide 30 by 200 and multiply by 100: (30 ÷ 200) × 100 = 15%. So 30 is 15% of 200.
