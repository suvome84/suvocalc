Overtime Pay Calculator

The Overtime Pay Calculator works out your total pay for a period, combining regular hours at your normal hourly rate with overtime hours paid at a higher multiplier.

What it is used for

It is used by hourly workers and their employers to check a payslip, to estimate weekly or monthly earnings when extra hours are worked, and to compare overtime rates such as 1.5× and 2×.

Total Pay (Regular + Overtime)

—

Formula

Regular Pay = Hourly Rate × Regular Hours. Overtime Pay = Hourly Rate × Overtime Multiplier × Overtime Hours. Total Pay = Regular Pay + Overtime Pay. Overtime hourly rate = Hourly Rate × Multiplier.

Worked example

Inputs: Hourly Rate = ₹500, Regular Hours Worked = 40, Overtime Hours = 10, Overtime Multiplier = 1.5. Step 1: regular pay = 500 × 40 = ₹20,000. Step 2: overtime rate = 500 × 1.5 = ₹750 per hour. Step 3: overtime pay = 750 × 10 = ₹7,500. Step 4: total pay = 20,000 + 7,500 = ₹27,500 for 50 hours worked.

How to use

  1. Step 1: Enter your normal pay per hour in the "Hourly Rate (₹)" field.
  2. Step 2: Enter your normal working hours in the "Regular Hours Worked" field.
  3. Step 3: Enter the extra hours in the "Overtime Hours" field.
  4. Step 4: Enter the overtime multiplier in the "Overtime Multiplier" field — 1.5 is common, 2 is double time.
  5. Step 5: Tap Calculate to see your regular pay, overtime pay, overtime rate, and total pay.

Tips

  • Confirm your overtime multiplier with your employer before calculating — 1.5× is common, but some jobs pay 2× on Sundays or public holidays.
  • Keep a simple record of your overtime hours each week so you can check the payslip against this calculator.
  • Overtime pay is taxable income, so the amount you take home will be less than the gross total shown here.
  • If your employer pays a flat overtime amount per hour instead of a multiplier, divide that amount by your hourly rate to find the equivalent multiplier.

Common mistakes

  • Counting overtime hours as regular hours as well, which double-counts them.
  • Using the monthly salary instead of the hourly rate — divide your monthly pay by your monthly working hours first.
  • Using the wrong multiplier, such as 1.5 when your employer pays double time.
  • Forgetting that the multiplier must be between 1 and 5 in this calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What does a 1.5× overtime multiplier mean?

It means each overtime hour is paid at one-and-a-half times your normal hourly rate. At ₹500 per hour, overtime pays ₹750 per hour.

How is overtime pay calculated in India?

Under the Factories Act, overtime is generally paid at twice the ordinary rate for hours worked beyond the legal limit, but many employers and contracts use 1.5×. Check your employment contract or company policy for the rate that applies to you.

If I work 40 regular hours and 10 overtime hours at ₹500 with a 1.5× multiplier, what is my total pay?

Regular pay is ₹20,000 and overtime pay is ₹7,500, so the total is ₹27,500.
