Attendance Calculator

The Attendance Calculator checks whether your attendance meets the required percentage, and tells you how many classes you can miss — or how many in a row you must attend to catch up.

What it is used for

It is used by college students to check if they meet the minimum attendance rule, plan how many classes they can safely miss, or work out how many classes they must attend to reach the required percentage.

Your Attendance

—

Formula

Current attendance % = classes attended ÷ total classes held × 100. If current % ≥ required %, classes you can miss = the largest number m where attended ÷ (total + m) × 100 stays at or above the required %. If current % < required %, classes you must attend = the smallest number a where (attended + a) ÷ (total + a) × 100 reaches the required %.

Worked example

Inputs: Total Classes Held 80, Classes Attended 55, Required % 75. Step 1: current percentage = 55 ÷ 80 × 100 = 68.8%. Step 2: 68.8% is below the required 75%, so the status is SHORTAGE. Step 3: find how many consecutive classes must be attended: (55 + a) ÷ (80 + a) ≥ 0.75. Solving gives a ≥ 20. Check: (55 + 20) ÷ (80 + 20) = 75 ÷ 100 = 75%. Final result: 68.8%, SHORTAGE — you must attend the next 20 classes in a row to reach 75%.

How to use

  1. Enter the number of classes held so far in the Total Classes Held field, for example 80.
  2. Enter how many of those you attended in the Classes Attended field, for example 55.
  3. Enter the required percentage in the Required % field — it defaults to 75.
  4. Tap Calculate to see your current percentage and SAFE or SHORTAGE status.
  5. Read the result: either how many classes you can still miss, or how many in a row you must attend.

Tips

  • Update the numbers weekly so the can-miss figure stays accurate.
  • If you are in shortage, attend every class until you are back above the requirement — skipping even one makes the gap bigger.
  • Count practical and lab classes separately if your college tracks them separately.
  • Keep your own tally of attended classes; attendance sheets are sometimes wrong, and your own count is your evidence.

Common mistakes

  • Entering attended classes greater than total classes, which the calculator rejects.
  • Using the total for the whole semester instead of the classes held so far.
  • Leaving the required percentage at 75 when your college requires a different figure.
  • Counting holidays or cancelled classes as held classes.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 75% the default?

Most colleges in India require 75%, and this tool uses 75 as its default. But the real rule is your college's — enter your own required percentage if it is different.

What does it show when my attendance is already safe?

If you are above the requirement, the tool tells you how many more classes you can miss and still stay above it.

Does it work for any subject?

Yes, as long as every class counts the same. If your college weighs theory and practical classes differently, the result is only an estimate.
