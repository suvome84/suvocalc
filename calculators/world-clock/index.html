World Clock

The World Clock shows the live current time in 10 major cities around the world, updating every second.

What it is used for

It is used to see the current time in major world cities at a glance, before calling, messaging, or scheduling across countries.

Formula

Method: the clock takes the current moment and displays it in each city's time zone, using the device's time-zone database. Each city's time = the current UTC time + that city's current offset from UTC.

Worked example

Example reading: when it is 15:30 in Kolkata (IST, UTC+5:30), the clock shows 10:00 in London (GMT, UTC+0), 05:00 in New York (EST, UTC−5), and 02:00 in Los Angeles (PST, UTC−8). Step 1: subtract 5 hours 30 minutes from 15:30 to get 10:00 UTC. Step 2: New York is 5 hours behind UTC in standard time, so 10:00 − 5:00 = 05:00. Step 3: Los Angeles is 8 hours behind UTC, so 10:00 − 8:00 = 02:00. Final result: the same moment reads 15:30 in Kolkata, 10:00 in London, 05:00 in New York, and 02:00 in Los Angeles.

How to use

  1. Open the World Clock tool — no inputs are needed.
  2. Find your city in the list of 10 major cities.
  3. Compare its time with the city you need.
  4. Remember the day can differ: a city ahead of you may already be on tomorrow.
  5. Refresh or reopen the tool if you need the latest time after a long gap.

Tips

  • For a quick meeting check, find both cities in the list and read the hour difference directly.
  • Kolkata (IST) is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of UTC and never changes for daylight saving.
  • If a city is 10 or more hours ahead, the other person may already be on the next day.
  • Use the Time Zone Converter when you need to convert one specific meeting time rather than just seeing the current time.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting that the date can be different in another city, not just the hour.
  • Using the clock to schedule a meeting without checking whether the other city is on daylight saving time.
  • Assuming all cities in one country share a time — the USA alone has several zones.
  • Reading a stale screenshot of the clock instead of opening it live.

Frequently asked questions

Which cities does the World Clock show?

Ten: New York, London, Dubai, Kolkata, Dhaka, Singapore, Tokyo, Sydney, Los Angeles, and Paris.

Does it update live?

Yes. The times refresh every second, so the clock keeps moving while the tool is open.

What happens during daylight saving time?

During daylight saving time, London, New York, and Los Angeles shift by an hour. The tool reads your device's time-zone data, so it follows the current rules for each city.
