Body Fat % Calculator

The Body Fat Calculator estimates your body fat percentage from a few tape measurements, using the US Navy formula.

What it is used for

It is used to estimate how much of your body weight is fat versus lean mass, and to track changes in body composition over time without any special equipment.

Body Fat %

—

Formula

Men: Body fat % = 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log10(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log10(height)) − 450. Women: Body fat % = 495 ÷ (1.29579 − 0.35004 × log10(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log10(height)) − 450. All measurements in centimetres.

Worked example

Inputs: Gender Male, Height 175 cm, Waist 85 cm, Neck 38 cm. Step 1: waist − neck = 85 − 38 = 47. Step 2: apply the US Navy formula: 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log10(47) + 0.15456 × log10(175)) − 450 = 16.9%. Final result: about 16.9% body fat, which the calculator places in the Fitness category for men (14–18%). This is an estimate, not a medical measurement.

How to use

  1. Choose your gender from the Gender dropdown.
  2. Enter your height in the Height (cm) field.
  3. Measure your waist in the Waist (cm) field, at the narrowest point or at the navel, without pulling the tape tight.
  4. Measure your neck in the Neck (cm) field, just below the Adam's apple.
  5. Women also enter the Hip (cm) measurement; men can leave it blank. Tap Calculate to see the result and category.

Tips

  • Measure in the morning, standing relaxed, with the tape level and snug but not squeezing.
  • Take each measurement twice and use the average if the two readings differ.
  • Use the same measuring points every time so changes over weeks are comparable.
  • Track the trend over months rather than worrying about a single reading.
  • For a precise body fat measurement, methods like a DEXA scan are far more accurate than any tape formula — and this result is an estimate, not medical advice.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring the waist over thick clothing or while sucking in your stomach.
  • Measuring the neck too high or too low, which changes the result noticeably.
  • Entering inches instead of centimetres.
  • Women skipping the hip measurement, which the women's formula needs.

Frequently asked questions

What body fat percentage is healthy?

The calculator's male categories are: below 6% Essential Fat, 6–14% Athlete, 14–18% Fitness, 18–25% Average, above 25% Obese. For women: below 14% Essential, 14–21% Athlete, 21–25% Fitness, 25–32% Average, above 32% Obese. These are general guides, not medical advice.

How accurate is the US Navy formula?

It is a rough estimate from tape measurements. It is useful for tracking change over time, but it can differ from lab methods by several percentage points.

Why does the women's formula need a hip measurement?

Because fat distribution differs between men and women, the women's version of the formula includes the hip circumference to improve the estimate.
