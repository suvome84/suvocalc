What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) = fixed monthly payment = Principal portion + Interest portion.

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n - 1)

P = Loan amount · r = Monthly rate (Annual÷12÷100) · n = Months

Example: ₹30 Lakh Home Loan at 8.5%, 20 Years

  • EMI = ₹26,035/month
  • Total paid = ₹62.5 Lakhs
  • Interest paid = ₹32.5 Lakhs (!)
Reality Check

On a 20-year home loan at 8.5%, you pay MORE as interest than the principal amount!

Common Mistakes

  • Not comparing banks — 0.25% difference saves lakhs
  • Taking maximum loan amount — borrow only what you need
  • Ignoring processing fees (0.5-2% of loan)
