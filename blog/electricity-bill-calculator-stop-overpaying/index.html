Bill = Units Used × Rate/Unit + Fixed Charges + Taxes

Units = Watts ÷ 1000 × Hours. Example: 1000W AC × 8hrs = 8 units/day = 240 units/month.

5 Ways to Cut Bill

  1. Set AC to 24-26°C (each degree lower adds 6% to bill)
  2. Use LED bulbs (80% less than incandescent)
  3. Unplug chargers when not in use (standby power!)
  4. Use ceiling fan with AC (allows 2°C higher AC setting)
  5. Turn off geyser 10 mins before use — it retains heat
