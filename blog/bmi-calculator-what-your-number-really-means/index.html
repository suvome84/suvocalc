What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) estimates whether you are in a healthy weight range based on height and weight.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m)

Example: 70kg, 1.75m → BMI = 70 ÷ 3.06 = 22.9 (Normal ✅)

BMI Categories (WHO)

Limitation

BMI does not distinguish fat vs muscle. Athletes often show "Overweight" BMI despite being very fit.

Healthy Weight for Height 1.70m

53.5 kg – 71.9 kg (BMI 18.5–24.9)

Action Plan

  • Underweight: Add 300-500 extra cal/day, focus on protein
  • Overweight: 500 cal/day deficit, 150 mins cardio/week
  • Normal: Maintain! Exercise 3-5x/week, sleep 7-8 hrs
