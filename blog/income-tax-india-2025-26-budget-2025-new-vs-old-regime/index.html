New vs Old Regime

Since 2023-24, New Tax Regime is the default. You must actively opt for Old Regime if you want it.

New Tax Regime Slabs 2024-25

Income SlabRate
Up to ₹3LNil
₹3L–₹7L5%
₹7L–₹10L10%
₹10L–₹12L15%
₹12L–₹15L20%
Above ₹15L30%
87A Rebate

Zero tax if total income ≤ ₹7 Lakh under New Regime!

Old Regime Slabs

Income SlabRate
Up to ₹2.5LNil

₹5L–₹10L20%
Above ₹10L30%

Which Regime to Choose?

Simple rule: If your total deductions exceed ₹3.5 Lakh, Old Regime likely saves more. Otherwise, New Regime is better.

Do Not Miss

₹50,000 Standard Deduction available in BOTH regimes. Always claim it.

Filing Deadlines

  • 31st July — Salaried (no audit)
  • 31st October — Business/professionals with audit
  • 31st December — Belated return (with penalty)
