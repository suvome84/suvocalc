Why Strong Passwords?

In 2023, over 4.5 billion passwords were leaked. Most hacked accounts used weak or reused passwords.

Top 3 Most Common

123456, password, qwerty — these are cracked instantly.

Password Cracking Speed

LengthTypeTime to Crack
6 charslowercase onlyInstantly
8 charsmixed8 hours
12 charsall types3,000 years
16 charsall typesBillions of years
Goal

Use 16+ characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Practically unbreakable.

Rules to Follow

  • ✅ Different password for every account
  • ✅ Never use personal info (birthdate, name)
  • ✅ Use a password manager (Bitwarden is free)
  • ✅ Enable 2FA everywhere
  • ❌ Never store passwords in notes or spreadsheets
