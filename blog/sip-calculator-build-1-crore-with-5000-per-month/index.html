What is SIP?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) = investing a fixed amount monthly in mutual funds. Like an automatic savings habit that grows with the market.

FV = P × [((1+r)^n - 1) / r] × (1+r)

P = monthly amount · r = monthly rate (annual÷12÷100) · n = months

Key Insight

SIP is a METHOD of investing, not a product. You can SIP in equity, debt, or hybrid mutual funds.

Common Mistakes

  • Stopping SIP during market crashes (worst time to stop)
  • Starting too late — every year of delay costs lakhs
  • Not increasing SIP annually with salary hike

Pro Tips

  • Start with any amount — even ₹500/month
  • Increase SIP by 10% every year (Step-Up SIP)
  • Don't pause during market dips — buy more units at lower price
