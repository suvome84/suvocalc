What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) ranges from 0 to 10. It represents overall academic performance across all semesters.

CGPA = Sum of (SGPA × Credits) ÷ Total Credits

UGC Standard Formula

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
