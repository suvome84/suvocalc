Percentage: 7 Everyday Uses You Must Know

3 min read

Table of Contents
Basic FormulaShoppingExamsSalary HikeInvestment ReturnsRestaurant TipMental Math Trick
X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

1. Shopping Discounts

₹2000 item, 30% off: Save = 30% of 2000 = ₹600. Pay = ₹1,400

2. Exam Marks

456/600: % = (456 ÷ 600) × 100 = 76%

3. Salary Hike

₹35,000 salary, 15% hike: Hike = ₹5,250. New salary = ₹40,250

4. Investment Returns

Bought ₹100, sold ₹145: Return = 45% profit

5. Restaurant Tip

Bill ₹850, 15% tip = ₹127.50

6. Discount Stacking Warning

Common Mistake

10% off then 20% off ≠ 30% off! Actual discount = 10% + 20% of remaining 90% = 28% only.

Mental Math

10% = move decimal left. 10% of ₹1,500 = ₹150. Then: 30% = 3×150 = ₹450
