Fixed 25-minute focused work + 5-minute breaks.</p><div class="formula-box"><code>25 min FOCUS → 5 min BREAK → (×4) → 30 min LONG BREAK</code></div><h2>Why It Works</h2><ul><li><strong>Parkinson's Law:</strong> Work expands to fill time. 25-min deadline creates urgency.</li><li><strong>Attention span:</strong> Human focus peaks at 20-25 minutes.</li><li><strong>Dopamine breaks:</strong> Short rewards reinforce the work habit.</li></ul><div class="tip-box tip"><div class="tb-lbl"><i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb"></i>Phone Rule</div><p>Put your phone in another room during the 25 minutes. Notifications drop productivity by 40% even if you do not check them.</p></div><h2>For Students: Best Practices</h2><ul><li>Start with the hardest subject (fresh brain = better performance)</li><li>Use breaks to review previous session notes</li><li>Plan: 1 Pomodoro = 1 chapter</li><li>Aim for 8-12 Pomodoros/day during exam prep</li></ul><div class="tip-box ok"><div class="tb-lbl"><i class="fa-solid fa-circle-check"></i>Result</div><p>Studies show Pomodoro increases productivity by 40-80% vs unstructured study sessions.</p></div>
