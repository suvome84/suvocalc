Enter the angle first, then press the function.</p><div class="formula-box"><code>sin(30°) = 0.5 &nbsp; cos(60°) = 0.5 &nbsp; tan(45°) = 1</code></div><div class="tip-box def"><div class="tb-lbl"><i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb"></i>Remember</div><p>SOH-CAH-TOA: Sine=Opposite/Hypotenuse, Cosine=Adjacent/Hypotenuse, Tangent=Opposite/Adjacent</p></div><h2>log & ln</h2><p><strong>log</strong> = base-10. log(100)=2, log(1000)=3. <strong>ln</strong> = natural log. ln(e)=1.</p><div class="tip-box warn"><div class="tb-lbl"><i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation"></i>Common Mistake</div><p>Never take log of 0 or a negative number — result is undefined.</p></div><h2>Powers & Roots</h2><p>Use <strong>xʸ</strong>: Enter 2 → xʸ → 10 → = → 1024.</p><div class="formula-box"><code>2^10 = 1024 &nbsp; √144 = 12</code></div><h2>Factorial n!</h2><p>5! = 120. 10! = 3,628,800. Used in probability. Tip: 20! has 18 digits!</p><div class="tip-box ok"><div class="tb-lbl"><i class="fa-solid fa-circle-check"></i>Pro Tip</div><p>Use x² for squaring (faster). Use 1/x for reciprocal. π = 3.14159..., e = 2.71828...</p></div>
