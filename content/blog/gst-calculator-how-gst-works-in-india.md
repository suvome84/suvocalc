<div class="blog-toc"><div class="blog-toc-ttl">Table of Contents</div><a href="#">What is GST?</a><a href="#">GST Calculation</a><a href="#">GST Rates</a><a href="#">Common Items</a><a href="#">Reverse GST</a></div><h2>What is GST?</h2><p>GST (Goods and Services Tax) — India's unified indirect tax since 1 July 2017. Replaced 17+ central and state taxes.</p><p><strong>GST 2.0 (22 September 2025):</strong> the 12% and 28% slabs were abolished. Current slabs are 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%.</p><div class="formula-box"><code>GST Amount = Price × GST% ÷ 100</code></div><div class="formula-box"><code>Total = Price + GST Amount</code></div><div class="formula-box"><code>CGST = GST ÷ 2 · SGST = GST ÷ 2 (within a state)</code></div><div class="formula-box"><code>IGST = full GST amount (between states)</code></div><p>Example: ₹1,000 item + 18% GST = ₹180 GST = <strong>₹1,180 total</strong> (CGST ₹90 + SGST ₹90 within a state).</p><h2>Reverse (find price from GST-inclusive amount)</h2><div class="formula-box"><code>Original = Total ÷ (1 + GST%/100)</code></div><h2>GST Rate Slabs</h2><table><tr><th>Rate</th><th>Items</th></tr><tr><td>0%</td><td>Essential food, books, milk, vegetables</td></tr><tr><td>5%</td><td>Household items, footwear under ₹1000, restaurants (no input tax credit)</td></tr><tr><td>18%</td><td>Most services, electronics, restaurants with AC/bar</td></tr><tr><td>40%</td><td>Luxury cars, tobacco, pan masala, aerated drinks (demerit rate)</td></tr></table><div class="tip-box tip"><div class="tb-lbl"><i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb"></i>Restaurant GST</div><p>Restaurants charge 5% (no input credit) OR 18% (with AC/bar). Check your bill!</p></div>
