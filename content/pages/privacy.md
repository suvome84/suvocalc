# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

## 1. Introduction

SuvoCalc ("we", "our", "us") at suvocalc.com is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information.

## 2. Data We Collect

**We do not collect personal data on our servers.** All calculation history, memory, and settings are stored locally in your browser's localStorage only — never transmitted.

## 3. Local Storage

We use `localStorage` to save calculation history, preferences, and memory — for example your theme, your favourite calculators, your calculation history, and your recently used tools. This stays on your device. Clear it anytime via your browser settings or the Clear button in the site.

## 4. Cookies

We use only a cookie-consent banner. No advertising cookies are set, because no advertising is running on this site yet (see section 5). If advertising is added in future, this policy will be updated before any advertising cookies are used, and the consent banner will ask you first.

## 5. Google AdSense

No AdSense is running yet. No ads are displayed on this site at present, and Google does not set advertising cookies through SuvoCalc. If Google AdSense is enabled in future, ads will be clearly marked as "Advertisement", Google may set advertising cookies per their [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and you will be able to opt out at [Google Ad Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). We do not control ad content.

## 6. Voice Input

Voice uses your browser's built-in Web Speech API only. We do not record or transmit audio.

## 7. Children's Privacy

SuvoCalc is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 8. Third-Party Links

SuvoCalc may link to third-party websites, for example Google's privacy and ad-settings pages. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites. Please read their policies when you visit them.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
