# About SuvoCalc

SuvoCalc is a free calculator website. SuvoCalc offers 57 calculators in one place — for finance, tax, investment, health, education, date and time, and everyday calculations.

## What SuvoCalc is

- **Free.** All 57 calculators are free to use. There is no payment, no subscription, and no paid tier.
- **No login, no account.** You do not need to sign up or sign in to use any calculator.
- **Calculations happen in your browser.** When you enter numbers, the calculation runs on your own device. Nothing you type into a calculator is sent to a server.
- **India-focused.** Amounts are shown in rupees (₹) and the tax and GST calculators follow Indian rules, including GST 2.0 (0%, 5%, 18% and 40%).
- **Built by an independent developer.** SuvoCalc is made and maintained by an independent developer, not a company. There is no office, no support team, and no team page — just the site and the person who builds it.

## What you can do here

Use any of the 57 calculators — EMI, SIP, income tax, GST, BMI, CGPA, electricity bill, loan eligibility, stock profit, and more — read the free guides in the Blog/Guides section, and add SuvoCalc to your phone's home screen from your browser menu — it installs as a web app, and after your first visit its calculators work offline.

## What SuvoCalc is not

SuvoCalc does not give financial, tax, medical, or legal advice. Results are estimates for education and everyday use. For important decisions, check with a qualified professional. See the [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/) for the full statement.
