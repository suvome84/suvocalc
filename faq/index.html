Frequently Asked Questions

Is SuvoCalc free?

Yes. All 57 calculators are 100% free. There is no signup, no payment, no subscription, and no hidden fee. Just open the site and start calculating.

Do I need an account?

No. SuvoCalc has no accounts and no login. You can use every calculator without signing up or giving any personal details.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Nothing you type into a calculator is sent to a server. Your settings, favourites, calculation history, and recently used tools are saved only in your browser's localStorage — on your own device. You can clear them anytime from your browser settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How accurate are the results?

Results are calculated with standard formulas and the rates and slabs shown on each page, and they are accurate for the numbers you enter. But they are estimates for education and everyday use, not professional advice. Rates, tax slabs, and GST rules change, and real-world amounts (bank interest, electricity bills, brokerage charges) vary by provider. For important financial, tax, medical, or legal decisions, verify with a qualified professional. See the Disclaimer.

Which calculators are included?

57 calculators in v31, covering finance (EMI, SIP, compound interest, salary, budget), tax (GST, income tax, HRA, home-loan tax benefit), investment and savings (stock profit, PPF, fixed deposit, CAGR, retirement, net worth), health (BMI, calorie, body fat, water intake, sleep, pregnancy due date), education (CGPA, grade, matrix, Pomodoro timer, attendance), date and time, and daily utilities (electricity bill, fuel cost, unit converter, QR generator, tip, speed, and more).

Does it work offline?

Yes, after you install it. Open SuvoCalc in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), add it to your home screen, and it installs as a web app (PWA). After installation, the calculators work without internet. See the guide Install SuvoCalc as a Free App — No App Store Needed.

Why is GST 2.0 shown?

Because GST changed. Under GST 2.0, in force from 22 September 2025, the 12% and 28% slabs were abolished. The current slabs are 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%. SuvoCalc's GST calculator uses these current slabs, with CGST/SGST/IGST split.

How do I save a result?

SuvoCalc has no account and no cloud saving, so results are not saved on a server. Your calculation history is kept only in your browser on your device. To keep a result, copy it from the result display, share it, or take a screenshot before you leave the page.

Who runs SuvoCalc?

SuvoCalc is built and maintained by an independent developer. It is a free calculator website, not a company, and there is no office or support team. See the About page.

How do I report a bug or suggest a new calculator?

Email ukin.in@yandex.com (as listed in our Privacy Policy), or use the Contact page. Please say which calculator you were using and what went wrong. New tools are added based on user suggestions.
